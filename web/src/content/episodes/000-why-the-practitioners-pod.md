Welcome to **The Practitioners Pod**.

There's no shortage of tech content. What's rare is hearing from the people who own the pager. They designed the pipeline, debugged it at 2am, and then had to explain the cost to finance.

That's who this show is for, and who it features.

## What we'll talk about

- **Data platforms that actually scale:** dbt, Snowflake, Airflow and the unglamorous plumbing between them.
- **AI in production, not in demos:** context governance, Model Context Protocol (MCP), agentic workflows and how to connect LLMs safely to governed enterprise data.
- **Engineering judgment:** build vs. buy, migrations, the trade-offs nobody writes down, and the incidents that changed how teams work.
- **Careers in the craft:** how senior ICs and leaders grow, and what they'd tell their younger selves.

## The one rule

Every guest has to have *done the thing*. No hype and no vendor pitches. Just practitioners talking shop.
